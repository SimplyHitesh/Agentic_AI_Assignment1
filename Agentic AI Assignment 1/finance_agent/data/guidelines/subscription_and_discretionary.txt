# Practical Savings Actions & Discretionary Expense Optimization

1. Subscription Purge:
   - Micro-transactions (₹119 Spotify, ₹649 Netflix, ₹199 YouTube, iCloud, Amazon Prime, gym memberships) create "death by a thousand cuts".
   - Actionable Step: Review bank statements for recurring monthly charges. If you haven't used a service in the past 14 days, pause or cancel it. Rotate subscriptions (subscribe to one video service at a time).

2. Food Delivery & Dining Optimization:
   - Ordering food via delivery apps carries hidden markups: menu prices are often 15-20% higher than dine-in, plus delivery fees, packaging fees, GST, and platform tips.
   - Actionable Step: Cooking batch meals on weekends cuts food spending by 40-60%. Limit takeout to celebratory occasions rather than convenience meals.

3. The 30-Day Impulse Buy Waiting Period:
   - For any discretionary gadget, apparel, or luxury spend exceeding ₹2,500, write it down on a wishlist and wait 30 calendar days. If you still want it and have saved cash for it after 30 days, make the purchase. 70% of impulse desires fade within a week.

4. Commute Optimization:
   - Routine cab hailing (Uber, Ola) during peak surge pricing often drains ₹8,000 to ₹15,000 monthly.
   - Actionable Step: Use metro or public buses for regular office commutes; book non-surge auto rides or carpool with colleagues for last-mile transit.
